about=HamQ brings an amateur radio station log into QGIS. Convert Maidenhead (QTH) locators to coordinates and back, search a locator from the toolbar and generate the Maidenhead grid (fields, squares, subsquares) as a vector layer. Import ADIF (.adi) logs into a GeoPackage without duplicates and see every QSO on the map as a point and as a geodesic path from your QTH, with distance and bearing. DXCC entity, continent, CQ and ITU zone are resolved from the callsign with the AD1C cty.dat country file, downloaded on first use (not bundled). A statistics panel shows QSOs, DXCC entities, continents, bands and modes. New QSOs from WSJT-X or JTDX arrive live over UDP and appear on the map within seconds. Optionally, Hamlib rigctld and rotctld show the radio frequency and mode and turn the antenna with a click on the map. The interface is in English and Serbian (Latin or Cyrillic script), switched with one click without restarting QGIS. No third-party Python packages are needed.
